## How this list is made

- Source: public job feeds of about 8,000 company career sites (applicant tracking systems), collected 19 November 2026
- Matching roles: titles mentioning DevOps, SRE, site reliability, platform, cloud, infrastructure, Kubernetes and related terms
- Roles posted in the last 60 days
- Removed: recruiting and staffing agencies and duplicate postings
